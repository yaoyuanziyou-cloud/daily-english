AI adds sparkle to synthetic diamond sector

Technology Source: China Daily ↗
Ready

In an age reshaped by artificial intelligence, diamond is emerging as a crucial material for cooling high-performance chips. At Computex 2026 in Taipei, MSI unveiled a new thermal solution for Nvidia's next-generation GPUs, featuring a diamond-copper composite baseplate. Nvidia announced that its next-gen Vera Rubin architecture GPUs will fully adopt diamond-copper composite thermal management. Lab-grown diamond plays a crucial role in the AI race to cool down chips that consume enormous amounts of power.

According to industry analysts, diamond is no longer an option but a necessity for cooling when single-chip power consumption exceeds 1,400 watts. Nvidia's Vera Rubin AI chip reportedly consumes a staggering 2,300 watts, equivalent to a household induction cooker running at full power. Without proper cooling, the chip's core temperature hits 110 degrees Celsius within minutes, crippling AI training performance. Traditional copper and aluminum heat spreaders cannot pull heat away fast enough, while liquid cooling alone is no longer sufficient.

Diamond has the highest thermal conductivity of any naturally occurring material, 5.5 times that of copper and 11 times that of aluminum. Experts say diamond-based chip thermal management is primarily suited for high-end chips used in the AI computing power sector.

在被人工智能重塑的时代，钻石正成为冷却高性能芯片的关键材料。 在台北举行的2026年Computex展上，微星为英伟达下一代GPU发布了全新散热方案，采用钻石-铜复合基板。 英伟达宣布其下一代Vera Rubin架构GPU将全面采用钻石-铜复合散热管理方案。 实验室培育的钻石在AI芯片散热竞赛中扮演着至关重要的角色。

业内分析师表示，当单芯片功耗超过1400瓦时，钻石不再是可选项，而是必需品。 据报道，英伟达Vera Rubin AI芯片功耗高达2300瓦，相当于家用电磁炉全功率运行。 如果没有良好的散热，芯片核心温度会在几分钟内飙升至110摄氏度，严重影响AI训练性能。 传统的铜和铝散热器无法足够快地导出热量，仅靠液冷也不再足够。

钻石具有所有天然材料中最高的导热率，是铜的5.5倍、铝的11倍。 专家表示，钻石芯片散热管理主要适用于AI算力领域使用的高端芯片。

EU nations cooperate on major drug seizures

World Source: China Daily ↗
Ready

Law enforcement officers from Italy, Portugal, and Spain have intercepted a high-speed boat in the Atlantic Ocean and seized 2.6 metric tons of cocaine. The collaborative operation also led to the arrest of four suspects of different nationalities. Italian officials said the drugs could have been sold on European streets for as much as 500 million euros. The wholesale value of the cocaine was estimated at approximately 78 million euros, but retail distribution would have generated far higher revenues.

Organized crime gangs are increasingly using extremely powerful rigid inflatable vessels known as go-fast boats. These boats can reach speeds of more than 70 knots, or 130 kilometers per hour. Crime gangs use the vessels to recover drugs wrapped in watertight packaging that has been dropped at sea by ships arriving from South America.

Portuguese police stopped the speedboat 90 kilometers southwest of Cape Espichel, near Portugal's capital Lisbon. Ecuador's president said around 70 percent of the cocaine flooding into Europe and the United States is shipped via his country. The European Border and Coast Guard Agency reported that other recent operations seized nearly half a metric ton of cocaine dropped in open water.

来自意大利、葡萄牙和西班牙的执法人员在大西洋拦截了一艘高速快艇，缴获了2.6吨可卡因。 这次联合行动还逮捕了四名不同国籍的嫌疑人。 意大利官员表示，这些毒品在欧洲街头出售可获利高达5亿欧元。 可卡因的批发价值估计约为7800万欧元，但零售分销将产生高得多的收入。

有组织犯罪团伙越来越多地使用被称为快艇的大功率刚性充气船。 这些船只速度可超过70节，即每小时130公里。 犯罪团伙利用这些船只回收用防水包装包裹的毒品，这些毒品由从南美洲抵达的船只投放在海中。

葡萄牙警方在首都里斯本附近的埃斯皮切尔角西南90公里处拦截了这艘快艇。 厄瓜多尔总统表示，涌入欧洲和美国的大约70%的可卡因通过他的国家运输。 欧洲边境和海岸警卫局报告称，近期其他行动在开放水域缴获了近半吨可卡因。
