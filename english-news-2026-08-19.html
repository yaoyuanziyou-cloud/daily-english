Unitree Robotics soars 629% to $62b valuation in share debut

Technology Source: China Daily ↗
Ready

Chinese humanoid robot maker Unitree Robotics surged as much as 629 percent in its stock market debut on Wednesday. The company achieved a market capitalization of nearly 445 billion yuan, or about 62 billion dollars. This valuation was more than seven times its IPO price.

Shares opened at 1,100 yuan on Shanghai's tech-focused STAR Market. The initial public offering price was 150.80 yuan per share.

Investors who were allotted 500 shares in the IPO would have made a paper profit of about 474,600 yuan.

The shares quickly pared some of those gains after the explosive opening. However, the stock remained up about 497 percent at around 899.60 yuan in morning trading.

中国人形机器人制造商宇树科技在周三的股市首秀中飙升了629%。 该公司市值达到近4450亿元人民币，约合620亿美元。 这一估值是其IPO发行价的七倍多。

股票在上海科创板以1100元开盘。 首次公开募股的发行价为每股150.80元。

在IPO中获配500股的投资者将获得约474600元的账面利润。

在爆发性开盘后，股价很快回落了部分涨幅。 然而，该股票在上午交易中仍上涨约497%，报约899.60元。

Germany demands Apple revise app consent prompts

World Source: China Daily ↗
Ready

US tech giant Apple is set to revise its policies on third-party developers' use of personal data for ad targeting on iPhones and iPads. Germany's competition watchdog concluded a multiyear probe into the matter on Monday.

Germany's Federal Cartel Office determined that Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework gave its own applications an unfair competitive advantage. The watchdog said Apple used more favorable and less discouraging language for its own apps than for third-party developers.

Apple has four months to roll out the changes, which will remain in effect for seven years.

The FCO president said Apple's rules must not treat its own offerings better than those of its competitors.

Under the commitments, third-party apps' consent pop-ups must be redesigned to be visually and linguistically neutral.

The changes will apply in almost all European Union countries.

Regulators in France and Italy have already penalized Apple for its ATT framework. France and Italy issued fines of 150 million and 98.6 million euros respectively.

美国科技巨头苹果将修改其关于第三方开发者使用个人数据进行广告定位的政策。 德国竞争监管机构在周一结束了对此事的多年调查。

德国联邦卡特尔办公室认定苹果的应用追踪透明度框架使其自身应用获得了不公平的竞争优势。 监管机构表示，苹果为自己的应用使用了比第三方开发者更有利、更少劝阻性的语言。

苹果有四个月的时间来推出这些变更，这些变更将有效七年。

联邦卡特尔办公室主席表示，苹果的规则不得使其自身产品优于竞争对手的产品。

根据承诺，第三方应用的同意弹窗必须重新设计，在视觉和语言上保持中立。

这些变更将适用于几乎所有欧盟国家。

法国和意大利的监管机构已经因ATT框架对苹果进行了处罚。 法国和意大利分别处以1.5亿和9860万欧元的罚款。
