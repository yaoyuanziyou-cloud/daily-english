Leading a Weekly Team Meeting

Business Scenario

Every Monday morning, Sarah gathers her team for the weekly status meeting. As the project manager, she believes that clear communication is the key to keeping everyone on the same page. She always starts the meeting by reviewing the action items from the previous week.

"Let's go around the table," Sarah says. "I'd like each of you to give a quick update on your current tasks." She makes sure to give everyone a chance to speak, and she takes notes while her colleagues share their progress. When someone raises a concern, she doesn't dismiss it. Instead, she asks follow-up questions to understand the issue better.

Today, David mentions that the design team is falling behind schedule. Rather than pointing fingers, Sarah stays calm and suggests a solution. "Why don't we prioritize the most critical features first? We can always come back to the smaller details later." The team agrees, and David looks relieved.

Before wrapping up, Sarah summarizes the key decisions and assigns clear next steps. "Great work, everyone. Let's touch base again on Wednesday to check our progress." She ends the meeting on a positive note, reminding the team that their hard work is making a real difference. A good meeting, she knows, should leave people feeling motivated, not drained.

Key Vocabulary
  • gather /ˈɡæðər/ — 召集，聚集
  • action items /ˈækʃən ˈaɪtəmz/ — 行动事项，待办事项
  • dismiss /dɪsˈmɪs/ — 不予理会，打发
  • prioritize /praɪˈɔːrətaɪz/ — 优先处理，确定优先顺序
  • critical /ˈkrɪtɪkl/ — 关键的，至关重要的
  • relieved /rɪˈliːvd/ — 宽憎的，松了口气的
  • summarize /ˈsʌməraɪz/ — 总结，概述
  • drained /dreɪnd/ — 精疲力竭的
Useful Phrases
  • on the same page
    达成共识，信息同步 — 开会时常说 "Let's make sure we're all on the same page."
  • go around the table
    轮流发言 — 会议中让每个人依次汇报时的常用表达。
  • point fingers
    互相指责 — 职场中应避免的行为："Instead of pointing fingers, let's find a solution."
  • touch base
    简短沟通，同步进展 — 非常高频的商务口语："Let's touch base next week."
  • wrap up
    总结，收尾 — "Before we wrap up, does anyone have questions?"
Pronunciation Tips
  • 连读练习："go around the table" 中 "go around" 可以连读为 /ɡoʊəˈraʊnd/，听起来更流畅自然。朗读时不要在每个词之间停顿。
  • 弱读注意："Let's" 中的 't' 在快速朗读时常弱化为喉塞音；"to" 在非重读位置发弱元音 /tə/ 而靤 /tuː/，如 "chance to speak" 读作 /tʃæns tə spiːk/。
  • 语调节奏：Sarah 的对话部分注意用升调表达建议语气（如 "Why don't we...?"），用降调收尾表达肯定（如 "The team agrees."）。朗读时模仿这种语调变化会让口语更地道。
