Chinese company showcases robots doing home services and logistics

Technology Source: China Daily ↗
Ready

At the ongoing 2026 World Robot Conference in Beijing, X Square Robot demonstrated how a unified general-purpose model drives applications across different scenarios, including home services and logistics. In the home service zone, the company's robots smoothly performed dozens of household tasks, including organizing clothes, cleaning, fetching deliveries, and even scooping cat litter. In one display, a dexterous hand powered by the company's WALL-B model unpacked a box, removed a fan, and switched it on.

In March, the company partnered with 58 Daojia to launch a robotic home-cleaning service, marking the industry's first large-scale deployment of robots entering home services. In May, the company launched the X Family Member Program, allowing robots to reside in users' homes for up to one month.

In the logistics zone, two robotic arms autonomously sorted real, randomly assorted packages with a sorting efficiency of 1,816 pieces per hour. The hardware solution costs 70 percent less than the humanoid robot approach used by peers. Despite handling real, irregular packages, the system maintained a 98 percent accuracy rate.

在2026年北京世界机器人大会上，X Square Robot展示了统一的通用模型如何驱动包括家庭服务和物流在内的不同场景应用。 在家庭服务区，该公司的机器人流畅地执行了数十项家务任务，包括整理衣物、清洁、取快递，甚至铲猫砂。 在一个展示中，由该公司WALL-B模型驱动的灵巧手拆开了一个盒子，取出了风扇并打开了它。

今年3月，该公司与58到家合作推出了机器人家庭清洁服务，标志着行业内机器人首次大规模进入家庭服务领域。 今年5月，该公司推出了X家庭成员计划，让机器人可以在用户家中居住长达一个月。

在物流区，两只机械臂自主分拣真实的随机包裹，分拣效率达到每小时1816件。 该硬件解决方案的成本比同行使用的人形机器人方案低70%。 尽管处理的是真实的非标准化包裹，该系统仍保持了98%的准确率。

Tougher social media restrictions test digital protection

World Source: China Daily ↗
Ready

Australia's social media minimum age law came into effect on December 10, banning children under 16 from using social media platforms. In June, the Australian government doubled the maximum penalty for breaches to 99 million Australian dollars, strengthening enforcement against platforms that fail to keep under-16s off social media. The new legislation also strengthened the powers of the eSafety Commissioner, making it easier to request information from social media companies to ensure compliance. Since the law took effect, more than 5 million accounts belonging to under-16s have been removed, deactivated or restricted.

The United Kingdom has announced plans to ban social media platforms from providing services to children under 16, with the measure expected to take effect next year. New Zealand, Norway and Turkiye are also considering similar restrictions.

Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese said big technology companies were not doing enough to comply, with too many children still accessing social media. The law serves as a protective line before children enter the complex online world, but parents also need to help children build stronger digital skills.

Closing an account does not make online risks disappear, as children can still connect through games, chat groups and other online spaces. Parents need to keep pace with the times and help children assess information, identify risks, and use technology responsibly.

澳大利亚的社交媒体最低年龄法于12月10日生效，禁止16岁以下儿童使用社交媒体平台。 今年6月，澳大利亚政府将违规最高罚款翻倍至9900万澳元，加强对未能阻止16岁以下儿童使用社交媒体的平台的执法力度。 新立法还加强了电子安全专员的权力，使其更容易向社交媒体公司索取信息以确保合规。 自该法律生效以来，已有超过500万个属于16岁以下用户的账户被删除、停用或限制。

英国已宣布计划禁止社交媒体平台向16岁以下儿童提供服务，该措施预计明年生效。 新西兰、挪威和土耳其也在考虑类似的限制措施。

澳大利亚总理安东尼·阿尔巴尼斯表示，大型科技公司做得还不够，仍有太多儿童在使用社交媒体。 该法律作为儿童进入复杂网络世界之前的保护线，但父母也需要帮助儿童建立更强的数字技能。

关闭账户并不能让网络风险消失，因为儿童仍然可以通过游戏、聊天群和其他网络空间进行连接。 父母需要与时俱进，帮助儿童评估信息、识别风险并负责任地使用技术。
