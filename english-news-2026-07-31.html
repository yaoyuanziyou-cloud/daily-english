Unmanned vessel sets milestone

Technology Source: China Daily ↗
Ready

The first domestically produced 100-metric-ton-level vessel in China has completed a round-trip autonomous navigation mission. The voyage covered 4,206 kilometers between Zhoushan and Sanya, setting a record for unmanned vessels on deep-sea voyages. The mission lasted 248 hours, with autonomous navigation accounting for over 96 percent of the time.

The vessel successfully passed tests involving high sea conditions, thick fog, and busy waterway traffic. Beikun Intelligence, the developer, said the vessel can automatically avoid collisions with incoming ships. The unmanned surface vessel maintains a safe distance of at least 0.55 kilometers from other vessels during navigation.

Core systems, including intelligent assisted navigation and energy management, were independently developed. Industry experts described the test as a major breakthrough in China's high-end marine equipment development.

中国首艘国产百吨级无人船已完成往返自主航行任务。 这次航行往返于舟山与三亚之间，全程4206公里，创下无人船深海远航纪录。 任务历时248小时，其中自主航行时间占比超过96%。

该船成功通过了高海况、浓雾和繁忙航道交通等多项测试。 研发方北鲲智能表示，该船能够自动避让迎面驶来的船只。 这艘无人水面艇在航行中与其他船只始终保持至少0.55公里的安全距离。

包括智能辅助导航和能源管理在内的核心系统均为自主研发。 业内专家评价，此次试验是中国高端海洋装备和智能航海技术发展的重大突破。

Chinese beauty brands enjoy a surge in popularity

Business Source: China Daily ↗
Ready

A growing number of foreign users are sharing their experiences with C-beauty, or made-in-China beauty products. Beauty influencer Mikayla Nogueira praised Flower Knows, a Chinese brand popular among younger consumers. On Xiaohongshu, a French woman named Sarah showed her collection of Chinese lotions, perfumes, and makeup.

Many consumers are attracted by the thoughtful integration of traditional Chinese elements in these products. Traditional ingredients such as ginseng and green tea are widely used in domestic beauty products today. A Brazilian student said the quality of Chinese beauty products matches that of famous overseas brands.

Florasis, another Chinese brand, is known for designs rooted in Chinese traditional aesthetics. Overseas consumers now view Chinese beauty as a cultural keepsake rather than just makeup.

The Chando Group operates in 15 countries and focuses on tech-driven skin care. Advanced manufacturing, including AI-powered workshops, is supporting the global expansion of Chinese beauty brands.

越来越多外国用户在社交媒体上分享他们使用中国美妆产品的体验。 美妆博主米凯拉·诺盖拉称赞了受年轻消费者欢迎的中国品牌花知晓。 在小红书上，一位名叫萨拉的法国女性展示了她收集的中国护肤品、香水和彩妆。

许多消费者被这些产品中融入的中国传统元素所吸引。 人参、绿茶等传统成分如今被广泛应用于国产美妆产品中。 一名巴西留学生表示，中国美妆产品的质量可与知名海外品牌相媲美。

另一个中国品牌花西子以其植根于中国传统美学的设计而闻名。 海外消费者现在将中国美妆视为一种文化纪念品，而不仅仅是化妆品。

自然堂集团业务遍及15个国家和地区，专注于科技驱动的护肤产品。 包括人工智能无人工厂在内的先进制造能力，正助力中国美妆品牌的全球扩张。
