World Humanoid Robot Games open in Beijing

Technology Source: China Daily ↗
Ready

The second World Humanoid Robot Games opened in Beijing on Saturday evening, attracting global attention to cutting-edge robotics. A record-breaking 2,056 robots from 666 teams are competing in categories including sports, emergency rescue and logistics.

In the 100-meter dash, a robot finished in just 9.39 seconds, surpassing Usain Bolt's human record of 9.58 seconds. A robot also set a new high jump record of 2.88 meters, a huge leap from last year's best of 95.64 centimeters.

The opening ceremony featured dozens of humanoid robots playing musical instruments alongside a human orchestra. Thousands of robots performed together, showing how machines can move in perfect coordination. Humanoid robots also danced and interacted with children, offering a glimpse of future human-machine harmony.

In a special match, robots played table tennis with famous Chinese athletes, including Olympic champion Ding Ning. Following the Olympic tradition, robots lit the symbolic flame to officially start the games. The games will run until Wednesday, giving visitors several days to experience the latest robotic technologies.

第二届世界人形机器人运动会于周六晚在北京开幕，吸引了全球对尖端机器人技术的关注。 来自666支队伍的2056个机器人创下了参赛纪录，比赛项目包括体育、应急救援和物流。

在100米短跑中，一个机器人仅用9.39秒完成，超过了尤塞恩·博尔特9.58秒的人类纪录。 一个机器人还创下了2.88米的跳高新纪录，比去年95.64厘米的最佳成绩有了巨大飞跃。

开幕式上，数十个具有灵活手指的人形机器人与人类管弦乐队一起演奏。 数千个机器人共同表演，展示了机器如何实现完美协调。 人形机器人还与孩子们一起跳舞互动，展现了未来人机和谐共存的愿景。

在一场特别比赛中，机器人与包括奥运冠军丁宁在内的著名中国运动员进行乒乓球对战。 遵循奥运传统，机器人点燃象征性火炬，正式宣布比赛开始。 本届运动会将持续到周三，让游客有数天时间体验最新的机器人技术。

Demand for widebody aircraft rising

Business Source: China Daily ↗
Ready

Airbus expects China's demand for widebody aircraft to grow strongly over the next five years. The growth is driven by both rising international passenger travel and expanding air freight services.

China Eastern Airlines recently agreed to buy 25 A330neo jets, while Air China ordered 15 A350-900s. Air China Cargo also raised its order for the A350F freighter to 10 aircraft in May. China is not only Airbus' largest single-country market, but also has enormous development potential.

Airlines are replacing aging jets with newer, more fuel-efficient aircraft to cut costs and reduce emissions. International routes carried more than 40 million passengers in the first half of 2026, up 5.7 percent year-on-year. Airbus estimates that about 170 widebody aircraft in China could need replacement between 2030 and 2035.

Air cargo has become a parallel source of demand, supported by cross-border e-commerce and high-tech exports. Airbus forecasts global air cargo traffic to grow 3.3 percent annually over the next 20 years. However, turning demand into deliveries may remain challenging due to Airbus' backlog of over 9,300 aircraft.

空客预计，未来五年中国对宽体飞机的需求将强劲增长。 这一增长得益于国际客运量上升和航空货运服务扩张的双重推动。

中国东方航空最近同意购买25架A330neo客机，而中国国航订购了15架A350-900。 今年5月，中国国际货运航空也将其A350F货机订单增加到10架。 中国不仅是空客最大的单一国家市场，而且发展潜力巨大。

航空公司正用更新、更省油的飞机替换老旧机型，以降低成本并减少排放。 2026年上半年，国际航线载客量超过4000万人次，同比增长5.7%。 空客估计，2030年至2035年间，中国约有170架宽体飞机需要更换。

在跨境电子商务和高技术出口的推动下，航空货运已成为并行的需求来源。 空客预测，未来20年全球航空货运量将年均增长3.3%。 然而，由于空客积压订单超过9300架，将需求转化为实际交付仍可能面临挑战。
