France comes under grip of 'unprecedented' fires

World Source: China Daily ↗
Ready

French President Emmanuel Macron has described the wildfires ravaging the country this summer as 'completely unprecedented'. The Gironde region, home to Bordeaux's famous wine country, has seen its most devastating fires since the 1940s, with more than 160,000 hectares already consumed by flames. Macron told reporters the situation had been 'stabilized' but warned it remains intense and requires extreme caution in the coming days.

Nearly 4,000 people were ordered to evacuate holiday homes and campsites around Lacanau on the Atlantic coast, as weather conditions threatened to worsen. French Prime Minister Sebastien Lecornu said that in nine out of ten cases, fires are caused by human carelessness such as discarded cigarette butts and unattended barbecues.

Four firefighters have lost their lives during the current outbreak, but their efforts have saved an estimated 200,000 people. Eric Brocardi, a firefighter spokesman, described the flames as being of 'exceptional intensity' but praised the firefighting strategy.

Similar wildfires are spreading in Spain, where fires near Madrid have forced around 60,000 residents to evacuate. Around 77,000 hectares — an area roughly the size of Hamburg, Germany — have already burned in Spain. Spanish Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez urged citizens across the country to exercise extreme caution, calling the coming hours 'difficult and complex'.

法国总统马克龙称今年夏天肆虐全国的野火「完全史无前例」。 以波尔多著名葡萄酒产区闻名的吉伦特地区遭遇了自20世纪40年代以来最严重的火灾，超过16万公顷土地已被火焰吞噬。 马克龙告诉记者火势已「趋于稳定」，但警告形势依然严峻，未来几天需保持高度警惕。

由于天气状况可能恶化，近4000人被命令撤离大西洋沿岸拉卡诺附近的度假屋和露营地。 法国总理勒科努表示，十起火灾中有九起是由人为疏忽造成的，比如丢弃的烟头和无人看管的烧烤。

四名消防员在本次火灾中丧生，但他们的努力挽救了约20万人的生命。 消防员发言人布罗卡迪形容火焰强度「异常猛烈」，但称赞了灭火策略。

类似的野火正在西班牙蔓延，马德里附近的火灾迫使约6万名居民撤离。 西班牙已有约7.7万公顷土地被烧毁——面积大约相当于德国汉堡市的规模。 西班牙首相桑切斯敦促全国公民高度警惕，称接下来的几个小时将「艰难而复杂」。

Chinese firms shift to proactive risk management amid global expansion

Business Source: China Daily ↗
Ready

Chinese companies are moving from reactive crisis response to proactive risk management as they expand globally, according to International SOS. There are now more than 50,000 Chinese business entities operating across 190 countries and regions worldwide. China's outbound direct investment reached 174.38 billion dollars in 2025, up 7.1 percent from the previous year.

Companies face compounding challenges including geopolitical conflict, cybercrime, extreme weather, and inadequate local medical services. A recent survey found that only 10 percent of organizations believed they could respond 'very quickly' to emerging risks.

Artificial intelligence is adding a new dimension to risk management — it can help verify information, but also enables the rapid spread of fabricated reports and fake videos. Nearly 60 percent of respondents see AI's greatest potential in validating information from multiple sources. However, over 40 percent said their organizations had been affected by false or misleading information involving health or security risks.

Cvete Koneska, global security director at International SOS, advised integrating AI into a human-led risk management model. Companies are urged to assess risks based on specific project locations and workforce distribution, rather than relying solely on country-level ratings.

据国际SOS称，随着全球化扩张，中国企业正从被动危机应对转向主动风险管理。 目前全球有超过5万家中国企业在190个国家和地区运营。 2025年中国对外直接投资达1743.8亿美元，同比增长7.1%。

企业面临复合型挑战，包括地缘政治冲突、网络犯罪、极端天气和当地医疗服务不足等问题。 最近一项调查发现，只有10%的组织认为自己能够「非常迅速」地应对新出现的风险。

人工智能为风险管理增添了新维度——它可以帮助验证信息，但也使虚假报告和伪造视频更容易快速传播。 近60%的受访者认为AI最大的潜力在于从多个来源验证信息。 然而，超过40%的受访者表示，其组织曾受到涉及健康或安全风险的虚假或误导性信息的影响。

国际SOS全球安全总监科内斯卡建议将AI整合到以人为主导的风险管理模式中。 企业被敦促根据具体项目地点和员工分布来评估风险，而不是仅仅依赖国家级风险评级。
