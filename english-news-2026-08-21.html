AI needs a symbiotic reset with humanity

Technology Source: China Daily ↗
Ready

As companies pour billions of dollars into artificial intelligence while cutting jobs, fears of human replacement are mounting. The 2026 Stanford AI Index shows that AI is reshaping the labor market without yet causing mass job displacement.

The rapid deployment of AI today is not aligned with human well-being. It is driven by an ideology that treats human beings as obstacles to be overcome. A dangerous myth lies at its core — that machines will soon surpass us in every cognitive domain, rendering human expertise obsolete.

This myth is already being used to justify mass layoffs of software engineers, writers, translators and analysts. Today's AI systems mimic certain cognitive functions, but they do not understand, reason or reliably replace human judgment. We need a different path: AI that collaborates with humans instead of replacing them. Specialized, transparent systems should be designed to augment expertise in fields such as medicine, engineering and scientific research.

History shows that humanity has capitalized on technological revolutions by balancing risks and benefits. We have a choice: continue down the path of replacement, or invest in a future where AI enriches human intelligence. The question is whether we will have the wisdom to steer AI toward a true symbiosis with humanity.

随着企业向人工智能投入数十亿美元的同时裁减岗位，人类被替代的担忧日益加剧。 2026年斯坦福AI指数报告显示，AI正在重塑劳动力市场，但尚未造成大规模失业。

当今AI的快速部署并未与人类福祉相一致。 它受到一种将人类视为需要克服的障碍的意识形态驱动。 其核心存在一个危险的迷思——机器将很快在所有认知领域超越我们，使人类专业知识变得过时。

这个迷思已经被用来为软件工程师、撰稿人、翻译和分析师的大规模裁员辩护。 当今的AI系统模仿了某些认知功能，但它们不能理解、推理或可靠地替代人类判断。 我们需要一条不同的道路：与人类协作而非替代人类的AI。 应设计专业化、透明的系统，以增强医学、工程和科学研究等领域的专业能力。

历史表明，人类通过平衡风险与收益来利用技术革命。 我们面临选择：继续替代之路，或投资一个AI丰富人类智能的未来。 问题在于我们是否有智慧将AI引向与人类的真正共生。

China and Switzerland conclude trade negotiations

Business Source: China Daily ↗
Ready

China and Switzerland have concluded negotiations to upgrade their free trade agreement, a move set to deepen bilateral economic ties. The agreement will provide businesses with greater certainty amid rising global trade tensions. Chinese Commerce Minister Wang Wentao and Swiss President Guy Parmelin jointly announced the completion of the negotiations in Bern.

The two sides began negotiations in September 2024 and held five rounds of talks. The upgraded agreement will provide a higher level of two-way opening-up in goods, services, investment and trade rules. China is ready to work with Switzerland to formally sign and implement the upgraded agreement as soon as possible.

Parmelin described the agreement as an important milestone in bilateral relations. He said it will provide a more stable, transparent and predictable institutional framework for business development. The agreement sends a positive signal of the two countries' commitment to openness and free trade. The original China-Switzerland FTA was signed in July 2013 and was China's first with a continental European country.

中国和瑞士已完成升级自贸协定的谈判，此举将深化双边经济联系。 该协议将在全球贸易紧张局势加剧之际为企业提供更大的确定性。 中国商务部长王文涛与瑞士总统帕梅林在伯尔尼共同宣布谈判完成。

双方于2024年9月开始谈判，并进行了五轮磋商。 升级后的协议将在货物、服务、投资和贸易规则方面提供更高水平双向开放。 中国愿与瑞士尽快正式签署并实施升级后的协议。

帕梅林将协议称为双边关系中的重要里程碑。 他表示，这将为商业发展提供更稳定、透明和可预期的制度框架。 该协议传递了两国致力于开放和自由贸易的积极信号。 中瑞自贸协定于2013年7月签署，是中国与欧洲大陆国家签署的首个自贸协定。
