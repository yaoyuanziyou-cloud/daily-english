Negotiating a Project Deadline with Your Manager

Business Scenario

Last Tuesday, I realized our team would not finish the client portal in time. The original deadline was set for the end of the month, and that was only three weeks away. I had two unfinished features and a long list of bugs that needed fixing. Instead of panicking, I scheduled a one-on-one meeting with my manager to discuss the situation.

I came prepared with a clear breakdown of the remaining work and honest time estimates. I told her, I would rather deliver a polished product a week late than a buggy one on schedule. She appreciated my honesty and asked me to suggest a realistic new deadline. I proposed extending the timeline by ten days and cutting two minor features from this release. We agreed to move the launch to the following Friday and to ship the smaller features later.

To make up for the delay, I offered to set up a brief demo for the client next week. My manager liked that idea because it would keep the client informed and engaged. After the meeting, I sent her a short follow-up email summarizing our agreement.

The experience taught me that proactive communication is always better than silent stress. Asking for more time early is far easier than apologizing for missed promises later.

Key Vocabulary
  • deadline /ˈdedlaɪn/ 最后期限，截止日期
  • estimate /ˈestɪmeɪt/ 估计，估算
  • propose /prəˈpoʊz/ 提议，建议
  • release /rɪˈliːs/ 发布，版本
  • proactive /proʊˈæktɪv/ 主动的，积极的
  • summarize /ˈsʌməraɪz/ 总结，概述
  • polished /ˈpɒlɪʃt/ 完善的，精细打磨的
  • engaged /ɪnˈɡeɪdʒd/ 参与的，投入的
Useful Phrases
  • one-on-one meeting
    一对一面谈，用于与上级或下属私下沟通敏感话题
  • follow-up email
    跟进邮件，会议或谈话后用来确认共识、留书面记录
  • to be far easier than
    远比...容易，用于对比两种做法的难易程度
  • deliver on schedule
    按期交付，强调按既定时间完成承诺
  • ship the features later
    将功能延后发布，软件开发中常用的分阶段上线说法
Pronunciation Tips
  • 连读：“would not finish”中的“would not”快速连读成 /ˈwʊdnət/，自然过渡到 finish。
  • 弱读：功能词 than、a、to、of 在句中通常弱读，例如 “a buggy one” 中 a 发 /ə/。
  • 重音：重读实词 proactive、polished、realistic 来传达重点，让听者感受到你的自信。
