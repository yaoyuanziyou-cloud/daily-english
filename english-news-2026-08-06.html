Chinese AI models gain favor overseas

Technology Source: China Daily ↗
Ready

Alibaba Group has launched its Qwen3.8-Max model with 2.4 trillion parameters, capable of analyzing 200-page financial reports and processing over 100 hours of video. The model has entered deep testing in Tesla's vehicle systems in China and is expected to power Tesla's in-car platform soon. More US companies are adopting Chinese AI models, which offer frontier-level performance at substantially lower costs.

In the latest Arena benchmark, Alibaba's Qwen models ranked just behind Anthropic's Claude series. Qwen3.8-Max is expected to become open-source next week, further expanding access for developers. Its API pricing is set at 12 yuan per million input tokens and 36 yuan per million output tokens in China. International pricing is about 40 percent and 24 percent of Claude Opus prices for input and output respectively.

According to OpenRouter data, weekly usage of Chinese LLMs was 14.1 times that of US models as of July. Coinbase adopted China-developed AI models and cut its AI costs by nearly half through intelligent routing and caching. Experts say companies globally are increasingly weighing performance against cost, privacy, and flexibility for AI workflows.

阿里巴巴集团发布了拥有2.4万亿参数的Qwen3.8-Max模型，能够分析200页的财务报告并处理超过100小时的视频。 该模型已进入特斯拉中国车载系统的深度测试阶段，预计很快将用于特斯拉车载平台。 越来越多的美国公司正在采用中国AI模型，这些模型以大幅更低的成本提供前沿级性能。

在最新的Arena基准测试排名中，阿里巴巴的Qwen模型仅次于Anthropic的Claude系列。 Qwen3.8-Max预计将于下周开源，进一步扩大开发者的使用范围。 其在中国的API定价为每百万输入令牌12元，每百万输出令牌36元。 国际定价显著更低，输入和输出成本分别约为Claude Opus价格的40%和24%。

根据OpenRouter数据，截至7月，中国大语言模型的周使用量是美国模型的14.1倍。 Coinbase采用中国开发的AI模型，通过智能路由和缓存将AI成本削减了近一半。 专家表示，全球企业越来越倾向于在性能、成本、隐私和灵活性之间权衡AI工作流程。

Is El Nino's economic risk too hot to handle?

World Source: China Daily ↗
Ready

A looming super El Nino is sparking warnings from economists about its potentially devastating impact on economies and societies. A key area of the Pacific Ocean reached its warmest and most intense level ever recorded for the 15th consecutive week. The El Nino weather event is expected to last through to spring 2027 and trigger extreme heat, drought, and rainfall. The WMO warned of drought and wildfires in Indonesia, India, parts of Australia, South America, and southern Africa.

The WMO Secretary-General said the event provides governments with a window of opportunity to anticipate risks and act. Investors and policymakers are concerned that climate shocks could reinforce inflationary pressures facing economies worldwide. A climate economist noted that heat kills both lives and livelihoods.

Research shows that just one additional day above 32 degrees Celsius lowers annual payroll by 0.04 percent. The economist said climateflation is a real phenomenon that can already be measured. Single heat waves lead to large spikes in prices of agricultural products, which reverberate throughout the economy.

迫在眼前的超级厄尔尼诺正引发经济学家对其可能对经济和社会造成毁灭性影响的警告。 太平洋一个关键区域的温度连续第15周达到有记录以来的最高和最强水平。 厄尔尼诺天气事件预计将持续到2027年春季，并引发极端高温、干旱和降雨。 世界气象组织警告印尼、印度、澳大利亚部分地区、南美洲和非洲南部可能出现干旱和野火。

世界气象组织秘书长表示，该事件为政府提供了预见风险并提前采取行动的机会窗口。 投资者和政策制定者担心气候冲击可能加剧全球经济已面临的通胀压力。 一位气候经济学家指出，高温既夺走生命，也夺走生计。

研究表明，每年仅多一天气温超过32摄氏度，就会使年工资总额下降0.04%。 这位经济学家表示，气候通胀是一个真实的、已经可以被衡量的现象。 单次热浪通常会导致个别农产品价格大幅飙升，其影响会波及整个经济。
