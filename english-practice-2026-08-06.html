Writing a Professional Follow-Up Email

Business Scenario

After an important business meeting, sending a clear follow-up email can make a strong impression. It shows that you are organized, reliable, and serious about the work you do.

Start your email with a polite greeting and a brief reference to the meeting. Then, summarize the main points that were discussed in a few short sentences. Be sure to mention any decisions that were made during the conversation. Next, list the action items and make it clear who is responsible for each task.

Use simple language and short paragraphs so that everyone can read it quickly. If you promised to share documents, attach them before you hit send. It is also helpful to include a clear deadline for each follow-up task. End the message with a friendly but professional closing sentence. Before sending, read the email once more to check for spelling mistakes.

A well-written follow-up email keeps the team aligned and prevents misunderstandings. It also builds trust with your colleagues and business partners over time. With a little practice, writing professional emails will become a natural habit.

Key Vocabulary
  • follow-up /ˈfɒləʊ ʌp/ 跟进
  • reliable /rɪˈlaɪəbl/ 可靠的
  • summarize /ˈsʌməraɪz/ 总结
  • decision /dɪˈsɪʒn/ 决定
  • responsible /rɪˈspɒnsəbl/ 负责的
  • deadline /ˈdedlaɪn/ 截止日期
  • attach /əˈtætʃ/ 附上
  • impression /ɪmˈpreʃn/ 印象
Useful Phrases
  • make a strong impression
    留下深刻印象 - 用于第一次见面或重要会议后
  • action items
    行动事项 - 会议后列出待办任务时使用
  • hit send
    点击发送 - 日常写邮件完成时
  • build trust
    建立信任 - 描述长期工作关系
  • keep...aligned
    保持...步调一致 - 团队协作时确保目标一致
Pronunciation Tips
  • 连读：follow-up email 读作 /ˈfɒləʊ ʌp ˈiːmeɪl/，up 和 email 之间轻微连读。
  • 弱读：for each task 中 for 弱读为 /fə/，不要读成重读的 /fɔːr/。
  • 重音：professional 重音在第二音节 /prəˈfeʃənl/，不要错放在第一音节。
