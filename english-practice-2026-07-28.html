Preparing for a Job Interview

Business Scenario

When you are invited to a job interview, it is normal to feel a little nervous. However, good preparation can help you feel more confident and perform better. Start by researching the company online and reading about its products, values, and recent news. This knowledge will help you explain why you want to work there. You should also review the job description carefully so you can match your skills to the role.

On the day of the interview, dress professionally and arrive at least ten minutes early. Bring extra copies of your resume and a notebook to write down important points. During the conversation, listen carefully and answer questions with clear examples from your past experience. It is also helpful to prepare a few questions to ask the interviewer about the team or company culture. Remember that an interview is not only a test; it is also a chance for you to learn about the employer.

After the meeting, send a short thank-you email within twenty-four hours. In the message, mention something specific you discussed and restate your interest in the position. This small gesture shows that you are polite, organized, and truly interested. Even if you do not get the job, every interview is a valuable learning experience. Stay positive, keep practicing, and the right opportunity will come.

Useful Phrases
  • feel nervous
    感到紧张 — 用于描述面对压力情境时的情绪，如面试、演讲前
  • match your skills to the role
    将你的技能与岗位匹配 — 面试中说明自己适合该职位
  • arrive early
    提前到达 — 面试或重要会议时展现守时
  • past experience
    过往经验 — 回答行为面试问题时引用
  • thank-you email
    感谢邮件 — 面试后 24 小时内发送以表达礼貌和持续兴趣
Pronunciation Tips
  • 连读：interview is 读作 /ˈɪntərvjuːɪz/，注意两词之间自然连读。
  • 弱读：can 和 for 在句中通常弱读为 /kən/ 和 /fər/，不要重读。
  • 重音：prePARation、proFESSIONally、opporTUnity 等词重音在第二或第三个音节。
