AI ecosystem lifts China's global position

Technology Source: China Daily ↗
Ready

Chinese researchers and entrepreneurs are playing an increasingly important role in the global development of generative AI. Cao Yanpei, chief scientist at Chinese AI company Tripo AI, said China's AI ecosystem enables ideas to move from academic papers to practical products quickly. Universities provide strong training and long-term research, while companies offer computing resources and large user markets.

Chinese AI talent has gained greater international visibility through participation in global conferences and open-source research communities. Tripo has released more than 30 open-source projects, including TripoSG, to help advance global AI research.

China's advantage in generative 3D AI starts with talent, as Chinese teams moved early in 3D generation and related research. The country benefits from a short path between digital creation and physical production, supported by large online creator communities.

China installed about 295,000 industrial robots in 2024, accounting for 54 percent of the global total. The growing robotics market creates a new need for consistent simulation using AI technology. Despite rising geopolitical tensions, Cao said international cooperation remains essential to advancing AI and computer graphics.

中国研究人员和企业家在全球生成式AI发展中扮演着日益重要的角色。 中国AI公司Tripo AI的首席科学家曹严培表示，中国的AI生态系统使创意能够快速从学术论文转化为实际产品。 大学提供强大的培训和长期研究，而公司则提供计算资源和庞大的用户市场。

通过参与全球会议和开源研究社区，中国AI人才在国际上获得了更大的可见度。 Tripo发布了30多个开源项目，包括TripoSG，以帮助推进全球AI研究。

中国在生成式3D AI方面的优势始于人才，因为中国团队在3D生成和相关研究领域起步较早。 在庞大的在线创作者社区的支持下，中国受益于数字创作和物理生产之间的短路径。

中国在2024年安装了约29.5万台工业机器人，占全球总量的54%。 不断增长的机器人市场为使用AI技术进行一致性模拟创造了新的需求。 尽管地缘政治紧张局势升级，曹表示国际合作对于推进AI和计算机图形学仍然至关重要。

Spain battles largest recorded wildfire as nearly 100,000 affected

World Source: China Daily ↗
Ready

Wildfires continued to rage across central Spain, with a blaze in Avila province becoming the largest on record in the country's history. The fire has burned around 50,000 hectares, while the total affected area across Madrid, Avila and Toledo has exceeded 77,000 hectares.

Nearly 100,000 people have been evacuated or ordered to remain indoors across 46 municipalities. Thousands of firefighters, supported by Spain's Military Emergency Unit and firefighting aircraft, are battling the blazes.

Officials said the fires in Avila and Madrid remained separate, connected only along a small and discontinuous front. Firefighters believe they have halted the spread of flames between the two fire zones, though the perimeter is not yet fully secured.

Minister Sara Aagesen described the wildfire situation as complex, with emergency crews operating on multiple fronts. Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez visited the affected areas and said the situation was evolving positively, with parts of the fires under control.

King Felipe VI and Queen Letizia also visited the disaster zone to express support for affected residents and frontline personnel. As of July 24, wildfires had burned a total of 172,453 hectares across Spain this year.

野火在西班牙中部持续肆虐，阿维拉省的一场大火成为该国历史上最大规模的火灾。 这场火灾已烧毁约5万公顷土地，马德里、阿维拉和托莱多三地的总受灾面积已超过7.7万公顷。

近10万人已被疏散或被要求在46个市镇内留守室内。 在西班牙军事应急部队和消防飞机的支援下，数千名消防员正在与大火搏斗。

官员表示阿维拉和马德里的火势仍然分开，仅通过一小段不连续的火灾前沿相连。 消防员认为他们已经阻止了火势在两个火区之间的蔓延，但火场周界尚未完全控制。

部长Sara Aagesen形容野火形势复杂，应急人员正在多条战线上作业。 首相佩德罗·桑切斯访问了受灾地区，表示情况正在朝着积极方向发展，部分火势已得到控制。

国王费利佩六世和王后莱蒂齐亚也访问了灾区，向受灾居民和一线人员表达支持。 截至7月24日，今年西班牙全国野火已烧毁总计172,453公顷土地。
